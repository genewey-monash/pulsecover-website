# Google Flow prompts for PulseCover

Brand palette: ruby red #B71234, warm gold #E8A317, charcoal #26242F, white. Photos = real people, photorealistic. Logo = the only animated asset.

## How to use
1. Open Flow, choose **Image** for the logo and photos, **Video (image-to-video)** for the animated logo.
2. Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** (photos) at the start of every photo prompt, then the specific prompt, then the **NEGATIVE** line (use Flow's negative prompt box if shown, otherwise append "Avoid: ...").
3. Generate 4 options, pick the best, upscale to the highest resolution, download PNG/JPG.
4. Name files exactly as in brackets so they drop straight into the site.

## STYLE BLOCK (photos)
Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph, shot on a full-frame mirrorless camera, 35mm lens, f/2.0, natural light, shallow depth of field, true-to-life skin texture with visible pores, natural proportions, anatomically correct hands with five fingers, candid unposed expression, warm cinematic colour grade with ruby-red and warm-gold accents in wardrobe or environment, clean uncluttered composition with space for text overlay, Malaysian setting, real people (not illustrated, not 3D, not animated). Any wearable device is a plain generic fitness band or smartwatch with a blank dark or softly blurred screen, no brand names, no logos, no readable text anywhere.

## NEGATIVE (append to every photo)
Avoid: text, letters, watermark, logos, brand marks, Apple Watch / Fitbit / Garmin / Whoop branding, cartoon, illustration, 3D render, CGI look, plastic or airbrushed skin, extra fingers, deformed hands, extra limbs, distorted faces, duplicated people, blurry subject, harsh flash, oversaturated colours, stock-photo clichés, hospital or medical setting, sad mood.

## LOGO (animated allowed)

### L1. Logo mark, static  [logo-mark.png]
Aspect 1:1. Flat vector style logo icon on a pure white background, centred, generous margin. A bold rounded shield outline in ruby red (#B71234). The shield's top edge flows into a single continuous heartbeat / pulse line (ECG wave) that crosses the shield from left to right with one sharp peak, the pulse line in warm gold (#E8A317), line weight consistent and thick, rounded caps. Minimal, modern, trustworthy, health-tech and insurance feel, geometric, symmetrical balance, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no text, no letters, works at 24px favicon size. Avoid: text, wordmark, gradients, shadows, bevel, photo texture, extra decorations.

### L2. Logo mark on dark  [logo-mark-dark.png]
Same as L1 but on a solid charcoal #26242F background, shield outline in white and the pulse line in warm gold. No text.

### L3. Animated logo  [logo-animated.mp4]  (Flow video, image-to-video, start frame = L1)
Duration 4 seconds, 16:9 canvas with the logo centred on a pure white background, 1080p. Motion: the ruby-red shield outline draws itself on from the top centre in a smooth stroke over 1.2 seconds, then the gold pulse line travels across the shield from left to right like a heartbeat monitor trace, with one small gentle bounce on the peak, then a soft gold glint sweeps once across the shield and the whole mark settles still. Smooth ease-in-out, calm confident pace, no camera movement, no zoom, no background change, no text appears, ends on the exact static frame of the logo so it can loop. Avoid: shaking, morphing into other shapes, particles, sparks, 3D rotation, text.

### L4. Animated logo on dark  [logo-animated-dark.mp4]
Same motion as L3, charcoal #26242F background, shield in white, pulse line in warm gold.
