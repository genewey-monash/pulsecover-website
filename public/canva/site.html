How your price is decided

No black box. Here is every rule, published in plain language.

1

A fixed base premium

Everyone starts from RM600 a year for a term life and health rider bundle. Age, gender and family history do not change it.

2

Four signals from your wearable

Steps (35%), exercise minutes (30%), sleep (20%) and resting heart rate (15%). Eight thousand steps a day and 150 exercise minutes a week earn full marks, so walking counts.

3

A capped band of ±15%

Your premium can go down by at most 15% or up by at most 15% versus the base, once a year at renewal. We keep it conservative so it is fair and actuarially defensible.

4

Renewal uses your 12-month average

One busy month, an injury or a trip cannot raise your price. Repricing happens only at renewal, never mid-year, and you are notified before it applies.

5

Pause tracking when life happens

Ill, injured, pregnant or travelling? Pause up to 60 days from your dashboard and those days are excluded from your average.

6

Your data stays yours

Opt-in only, encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold, never shared with employers. See our privacy page.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a medical exam?

No. Your wearable data replaces manual health declarations.

What if I do not own a wearable?

You can enter readings manually while you get a device. Wearable partners offer onboarding bundles for new policyholders.

Can my premium go up a lot?

No. The most it can move is 15% above the RM600 base, once a year at renewal, using a 12-month average.

I am under 18. Can I join?

Yes from age 16, with a parent or guardian named on the application.

Is there an upper age limit?

PulseCover is designed for every age group that uses a health tracker. Moderate activity like walking earns full marks.

Who is the actual insurer?

A licensed partner insurer (Insurer Name Sdn Bhd) underwrites your policy. PulseCover operates as its registered broker / MGA.

What does the app subscription add?

An optional RM5/month for deeper insights and coaching. It never changes your insurance price.

How do claims work?

File online, upload details, and follow each status live. No paper forms.

Which wearables work?

Fitbit, Garmin and Whoop, consolidated through Google Health Connect, on iOS and Android.

Privacy and data governance

Trust comes first. 77.8% of people worry about biometric privacy, so we treat governance as a precondition, not an afterthought.

1

What we collect

Only daily steps, exercise minutes, sleep hours and resting heart rate from your linked device, plus the details you give us to issue your policy. We do not collect location, messages or contacts.

2

How we use it

To calculate your health score and your renewal premium within the published ±15% band, and to give you in-app insights. Never to sell, advertise to you or share with employers.

3

Your controls

Sharing is opt-in. You can pause tracking, disconnect your device or request a copy or deletion of your data at any time, subject to insurance record-keeping law.

4

How we protect it

Encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, hashed passwords and a compliance lead who owns BNM and personal-data-protection obligations.

5

Fairness promise

We publish our pricing method, cap changes at ±15%, reprice only at renewal and tell you before. No algorithm change without notice.
